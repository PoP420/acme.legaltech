# Product Vision and Business Model

## Product statement

Acme LegalTech is a multi-tenant Contract Lifecycle Management platform for law firms and in-house legal teams that centralizes contract operations, enforces policy-driven review, and adds AI-assisted insights with human approval.

## Target customer segments

- Solo and small legal practices
- Mid-size law firms
- In-house legal departments

## Core value proposition

- One contract system for intake, review, obligations, renewals, and reporting
- Strong governance with role-based permissions and full audit trails
- AI assist for extraction, risk flagging, and search acceleration, with legal staff in control

## Business model options

### SaaS subscription tiers

- Starter: Core repository and workflows
- Professional: Playbooks, obligations automation, advanced reporting
- Enterprise: Extended controls, advanced integrations, and premium AI features

### Service add-ons

- Implementation and data migration services
- AI model tuning and policy setup
- Compliance and audit readiness package

## Product principles

1. Multi-tenant security is non-negotiable.
2. Human review remains the decision authority.
3. Platform behavior must be auditable and explainable.
4. Feature flags and packaging should support staged commercialization.

## Success metrics

- Contract intake to first review time
- Number of contracts managed per tenant
- On-time obligation completion rate
- Renewal miss rate
- AI suggestion acceptance rate
- User retention by tenant and role
