1. LegalTech: Contract Lifecycle Management (CLM) & Intelligence
This is arguably the best niche for an ABP + AI template. Law firms and corporate legal departments desperately need software to manage contracts, and AI has revolutionized this space.
Why ABP fits perfectly: Legal requires strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), deep Audit Logging, and Multi-tenancy (for law firms managing multiple corporate clients). ABP’s DDD approach handles the complex domain models of contracts, clauses, and obligations beautifully.
How AI fastens it:
AI Clause Extraction: Using LLMs to automatically extract key dates, liabilities, and renewal terms from uploaded PDFs.
Risk Scoring: AI analyzes contract language against a company’s standard playbook to flag risky clauses.
RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation): Lawyers can chat with their contract repository ("Show me all NDAs expiring in Q3 with a liability cap over $1M").
